## Brand & Style

This design system embodies an elevated collegiate aesthetic engineered for university faculty, researchers, and academic directors. Balancing the cerebral gravitas of classic publishing with the micro-precision of modern productivity tooling, the visual identity pairs literary serif typography with high-density, hyper-legible sans-serif operational interfaces.

### Core Tenets
- **Editorial Erudition:** The interface honors scholarly output. Large-scale structural elements, syllabus modules, and course titles convey the tactile calm of bespoke monograph publishing and archive reading rooms.
- **Instrument Precision:** Daily instructional workflows—lecture timelines, seminar sequences, learning outcome matrices, and assessment slippage—demand dense, distraction-free clarity akin to precision research instruments.
- **Calm, High-Contrast Restraint:** The visual atmosphere avoids synthetic neon gradients, frivolous illustrations, or aggressive consumer gamification. It relies on warm paper-like substrates, crisp architectural hairline dividers, and deliberate status accents.

### Design Movement
A synthesis of **Modern Editorial Publishing** and **Linear-Caliber Craft**:
- Paper-tinted neutral foundations over stark sterile whites.
- Subdued hairline borders (`1px`) establish planar structure in place of drop-shadow hierarchies.
- Typographic tension: warm, intellectual transitional serifs (`Newsreader`) framing disciplined, geometric sans primitives (`Plus Jakarta Sans`).

## Colors

The color palette draws inspiration from bookbinding buckram, archival vellum, and iron gall ink, modernized through an analytical productivity lens.

### Palette Architecture
- **Primary Canvas (`#FAF9F5`):** A warm off-white, light-absorbing ground that prevents eye strain during extended course construction and grading periods.
- **Card & Focus Surfaces (`#FFFFFF`):** High-clarity white planes reserved for primary editable documents, syllabus modules, and focused inspection sheets.
- **Secondary Surfaces (`#F2EFE9`):** Subdued grounding tone for sidebars, timeline headers, metadata rails, and table column bands.
- **Structural Lines (`#E4E2DC` / `#D4D2CB`):** Hairline precision borders defining modules, grid cells, and content regions without optical heaviness.
- **Primary Ink (`#0F172A` & `#18181B`):** Deep scholastic navy and slate-charcoal delivering authoritative contrast for headlines, navigation anchors, and dominant interactive actions.
- **Scholarly Status Accents:**
  - **Adaptive Sage (`#0D9488`):** Indicating syllabus pacing synchronization, validated learning milestones, and resolved semester dependencies.
  - **Academic Amber (`#D97706`):** Signaling schedule slippage, holiday calendar conflicts, and examination gaps requiring faculty review.

### Color Hierarchy & Usage
- Do not deploy saturated background colors across full page containers; color accents must be strictly reserved for indicators, timeline marks, micro-chips, and focus states.
- Text hierarchy must preserve contrast compliance: `#18181B` for body and headings, `#52525B` for secondary operational labels, and `#71717A` strictly for tertiary metadata, table timestamps, and auxiliary shortcuts.

## Typography

The typographic system creates deliberate structural tension between academic intellectualism and dense operational execution.

### Font Roles
- **Headlines (`Newsreader`):** Applied exclusively to course titles, syllabus segment titles, editorial viewheaders, and modal anchors. Set with slight optical warmth, normal to medium weights, and tight negative tracking to maintain intellectual gravity without feeling archaic.
- **Body & Controls (`Plus Jakarta Sans`):** Applied to core content items, session descriptors, inputs, navigational bars, buttons, and system badges. Crisp, geometric terminals provide high scanning velocity on 4K workstations and laptop screens.
- **Tabular Figures & Micro-Labels:** All dates, lecture module counters (e.g., `WK 04 / LEC 12`), grading percentages, and scheduling metrics must activate OpenType tabular numbers (`tnum`) and slashed zeros to ensure vertical alignment across data grids.

### Editorial Treatments
- Italicized styles in `Newsreader` are strictly reserved for academic course subtitles, book and citation references, or reflective instructional prompts.
- Uppercase styling is confined to `label-xs` tags (e.g., `SEMESTER 1`, `CORE PREREQUISITE`), tracking at `+0.04em` to retain micro-scale legibility.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout model is anchored by an asymmetrical multi-pane fluid workspace, tailored for complex curriculum modeling, schedule manipulation, and resource cross-referencing.

### Layout Mechanics
- **Multi-Pane Workspace:**
  - Left: Collapsible course/term navigator (`240px`–`280px` fixed).
  - Center: Fluid curriculum canvas (min `640px`, scaling to available width).
  - Right: Contextual metadata inspection drawer (`360px`–`420px`), docking seamlessly against the canvas boundary.
- **Grid Architecture:** 12-column responsive fluid grid with `1.75rem` (`28px`) gutters on desktop screens, stepping down to 8 columns with `1.25rem` on tablet viewports, and a single responsive column on mobile.
- **Rhythm:** Spacing follows an intentional 4px base increment (`0.25rem`), prioritizing compact micro-paddings (`space-sm` / `space-md`) within syllabus timeline nodes to maximize instructional density, offset by generous section breaks (`space-xl` / `space-xxl`) between major curriculum modules.

### Responsive Breakpoints
- **Desktop (≥ 1280px):** Full three-pane simultaneous density.
- **Tablet (768px – 1279px):** Inspection drawer shifts to an overlay slide-out; navigation collapses to icon + drawer rail; grid margins compress to `margin-tablet`.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** Linear sequence view; navigation shifts to contextual top bars and action sheets; font scales switch to `-mobile` variants.

## Elevation & Depth

This design system avoids theatrical elevation and artificial lighting effects. Depth is established through **tonal stratification** and **hairline separation**, reserving true elevation shadows for high-priority transient overlays.

### Architectural Tiers
- **Tier 0 (Root Canvas):** `#FAF9F5`. Continuous grounding surface for the entire viewport.
- **Tier 1 (Structural Containers):** `#FFFFFF` cards resting flush on the canvas, bounded by a continuous `1px` border of `#E4E2DC`.
- **Tier 2 (Recessed Utility Areas):** `#F2EFE9` containers inset into cards or sidebars to distinguish timeline headers, inactive lecture blocks, or formula engines. Bounded by `#E4E2DC`.

### Transient Elevation & Shadows
Shadows must never be used for static layout cards, buttons, or list rows. Elevation is strictly applied to detached floating surfaces:
- **Popovers, Datepickers & Menus:** `0 4px 16px -2px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.06), 0 2px 6px -1px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04)`. Outlined with a `1px` stroke of `#D4D2CB`.
- **Sliding Inspection Sheets & Modals:** `0 20px 32px -8px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.08), 0 8px 12px -4px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.03)`. Backed by an ivory/slate backdrop scrim (`rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.25)`) with an optical blur of `4px`.

## Shapes

The shape philosophy is crisp and architectural (`roundedness: 1`), signaling scholarly rigor and purposeful structure.

### Corner Radius Mapping
- **Base Elements (`0.25rem` / 4px):** Form inputs, dropdown selectors, timeline item blocks, table row selections, inline code blocks, and primary action buttons.
- **Intermediate Elements (`rounded-lg: 0.5rem` / 8px):** Syllabus segment cards, module grouping containers, modal dialogs, and slide-over drawers.
- **Extended Elements (`rounded-xl: 0.75rem` / 12px):** Dedicated workspace overview hubs and empty-state informational panels.
- **Pill Exception (`rounded-full`):** Exclusively designated for status chips, live pacing badges, and circular counter indicators. Never use pill shapes for standard text inputs or structural blocks.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Deep slate navy (`#0F172A`) solid background, `#FFFFFF` text, `0.25rem` border radius, `font-weight: 600`. Subtle hover shift to `#1E293B`. Zero shadow.
- **Secondary / Outline:** `#FFFFFF` background, `1px` solid `#D4D2CB` border, `#18181B` text. Hover state transforms surface to `#F2EFE9` with border darkening to `#18181B`.
- **Ghost:** Transparent background, `#52525B` text, `0.25rem` radius. Hover state applies `#F2EFE9` fill with `#18181B` text.
- **Sizes:** Compact default (`32px` height, `12px` padding-x, `body-sm`) for toolbars; Standard (`40px` height, `16px` padding-x, `body-md`) for primary view actions.

### Cards & Syllabus Modules
- Constructed on `#FFFFFF` with a `1px` border of `#E4E2DC`.
- Padding strictly bounded by `space-lg` (`1.25rem`).
- Header regions feature `Newsreader` titles paired with tabular status indicators and a drag handle trigger.
- Interactive cards receive no elevation lifts on hover; instead, borders transition crisply to `#0F172A` at `1px`.

### Badges & Status Chips
- Height: `22px`, font: `label-xs`, radius: `rounded-full`, padding: `0 8px`.
- **Synchronized / Pacing On-Track:** `#0D9488` text on an ultra-light tint surface (`rgba(13, 148, 136, 0.08)`), surrounded by a `1px` border of `rgba(13, 148, 136, 0.2)`.
- **Slippage / Revision Required:** `#D97706` text on `rgba(217, 119, 6, 0.08)`, bounded by `1px` border of `rgba(217, 119, 6, 0.2)`.
- **Draft / Inactive:** `#71717A` text on `#F2EFE9`, bounded by `1px` border of `#E4E2DC`.

### Timeline & Progress Tracks
- Syllabus track: `4px` continuous track line in `#E4E2DC`.
- Active curriculum completion filled with `#0F172A`.
- Slippage zones punctuated by `#D97706` patterned segments.
- Milestone nodes: `12px` solid circles with `2px` white inner borders resting on the track.

### Form Inputs & Selectors
- Background: `#FFFFFF`. Border: `1px` solid `#D4D2CB`.
- Height: `36px`, padding: `0 12px`, radius: `0.25rem`. Text: `body-md` in `#18181B`.
- Focus State: Replaces default browser outline with an assertive `1px` solid `#0F172A` border and `0 0 0 1px #0F172A` ring. No multi-color outer glow.
- Placeholder text: `#71717A`.

### Checkboxes & Radios
- Size: `16px × 16px`, radius: `2px` (checkboxes) and `rounded-full` (radios).
- Unchecked: `#FFFFFF` fill with `1px` solid `#D4D2CB`.
- Checked: `#0F172A` fill with a micro crisp white checkmark / center pip.

### Lists & Data Grids
- Hairline dividers: `1px` bottom border of `#E4E2DC`. Zero outer table borders.
- Header row: `#F2EFE9` background, `label-xs` typography in `#52525B`, uppercase with tabular alignment.
- Row hover: Subtle transition to `#FAF9F5`.
